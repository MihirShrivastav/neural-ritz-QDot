# Research Roadmap

The paper target is a neural-orbital CI framework for exchange, entanglement/correlation, and disorder sensitivity maps in semiconductor quantum-dot qubits.

Near-term milestones:

1. Validate v1 pair CI on analytic double-dot potentials.
2. Add detuning and barrier sweeps.
3. Add exchange sensitivity and resumable sweep manifests.
4. Add localized charge-sector and correlation observables.
5. Add simple disorder ensembles.
6. Add finite-difference baselines and convergence studies.
7. Add stronger entanglement metrics and publication figure recipes.

Claims to avoid in v1:

- replacement for full device TCAD,
- ab initio material accuracy,
- many-electron scalability,
- spin-orbit, valley, magnetic-field, or transport predictions.
